# France Top-10 Fitness Chains Interactive Atlas (2021–2026)

Interactive Leaflet catchment and expansion atlas of France's top 10 fitness chains (2,551 cleaned Metropolitan France records: 2,335 listed open, 192 closed since 2020, 24 pre-opening excluded from active counts), with year-end and current time slider (YE 2021 through November 2026), modeled IGN France 10-minute drive and 10-minute walk isochrones, and INSEE Filosofi 2021 demographics.

Live Map: https://brlaw777.github.io/france-fitness-atlas/
